🔒 Privacy & Data Ownership

PrepWise keeps three things: what is in your pantry, your recipes, and any food allergies you record along with how severe each one is. No name, no account, no contact details, no location — it never asks, so it never has them.

✓ Stored on your device: In this browser, on this device, and nowhere else

✓ No servers involved: There is no account to make and nothing to upload to

✓ Zero tracking: No analytics, cookies, or third-party tracking

✓ Yours to move: Export, back up, or delete the lot at any time

✓ No AI assistant unless you say so: On browsers that can offer PrepWise to an assistant, that is switched off until you turn it on yourself — see AI Agents in This Browser below

✨ Share with an AI Assistant

Produces a plain-English Markdown summary of your pantry, recipes, and shopping needs — formatted so an AI assistant can suggest meals from what you already have, work out substitutions, or plan a shop. Expiration dates are recalculated at the moment you export, so nothing is stale.

Where it goes is your call. Nothing is sent anywhere by PrepWise; you copy or download the summary and decide who sees it. Whatever you paste into an AI assistant goes to that provider. Two things are left out even then:

    🔌 AI Agents in This Browser

    Your browser can let an AI assistant work with PrepWise directly — ask what is in the pantry or what needs buying, hand it a photo of your fridge and have it offer to fill in your inventory, or have it write up a recipe with a timer on each step. This is off until you switch it on below, and stays off on every other browser you use PrepWise in.

    If you do switch it on:

    ✓ Answers are free, changes are not: An assistant can read your pantry. Anything that would change it opens the same preview a backup file gets, and waits for you

    ✓ It can add, never delete: A misread photo can suggest the wrong yoghurt. It cannot empty your pantry — that option is not offered

    ✓ Writing a recipe touches nothing else: An assistant adding a recipe is given no access to your inventory, so a recipe cannot quietly restock or spend your pantry

    ✓ Allergy severity stays here: Exactly as with the Markdown export, assistants get allergen names and never how badly each one affects you

    ✓ Still no servers: The assistant runs in your browser and PrepWise sends nothing. Whatever it reads goes wherever that assistant sends it, which is your choice of assistant

    Belongs to this browser, like the theme and text size, so it is not carried in backups.
